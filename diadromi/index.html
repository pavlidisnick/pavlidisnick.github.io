Diadromi

Trips, fuel and running costs for your cars and motorcycles, on your phone.

  • TripsRecord them with GPS, or add them by hand. Work or private, with a map of the route.
  • FuelFill-ups from a photo of the receipt, with consumption per tank.
  • Costs and remindersTolls, service, KTEO, insurance and road tax, and a reminder before each is due.
  • Stats and notesWhat each vehicle costs per month and per km, and a note on any record.
  • Yours onlyNo account and no ads. Everything stays on your phone, backed up to your own Google Drive if you want.
